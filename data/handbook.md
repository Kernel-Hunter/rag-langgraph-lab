## 1. About the course

1.1 Objectives. The course teaches how to write, test and evaluate prompts for
large language models, and how to connect a model to external knowledge with
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

2.1 Sessions. The course has 12 weeks. Each week has one 90-minute lecture and
one 90-minute lab. Labs take place in room B204.

2.2 Office hours. The instructor holds office hours on Wednesdays from 14:00 to
15:30. Book a slot by email at least one day in advance.

<!-- page 2 -->
## 3. Grading

3.1 Weights. The final grade is computed as: labs 30%, mini-project 30%,
final exam 40%.

3.2 Mini-project. Teams of two to four students build a RAG application on a
document of their choice. The project report is due at the end of week 11.
Late submissions lose 10% of the project grade per day.

3.3 Final exam. The final exam is a two-hour written exam held in week 13.
Notes are not allowed. A calculator is not needed.

<!-- page 3 -->
## 4. Attendance

4.1 Lab attendance is mandatory.

4.2 Absences. Two absences are allowed. A third absence without a medical
certificate means failing the course.

4.3 Late arrival. Arriving more than 15 minutes late counts as an absence.

4.4 Medical certificates must be handed to the administration within 48 hours
of the missed session.

<!-- page 4 -->
## 5. Tools and integrity

5.1 Allowed tools. Students may use AI assistants and coding agents for labs and
the mini-project. Every report must say which tools were used and for what.

5.2 API keys. Never share or commit an API key. A leaked key must be revoked
the same day.

5.3 Plagiarism. Copying another team's code or report gives a zero for the
whole mini-project for both teams.
